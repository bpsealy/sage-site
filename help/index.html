How Sage works

Sage learns from every hunt and fish you log, then scores your upcoming days against that history so you know when your spots are actually worth a sit. This page walks through every part of the app in detail, with real screens from Sage alongside each step.

Use the bar above to jump to a section, or just scroll — everything follows the order you'd naturally set the app up in.

Spots & Properties

Properties are the land you hunt — a lease, a family farm, public ground. Spots are the individual stands, blinds, or waypoints inside that land. Every spot keeps its own history, its own preferred wind, and its own score.

1 Open Spots & Properties

From the Hunting tab, tap the pin icon in the top right to open the map, or use the gear icon next to it for settings. Spots & Properties lives under the map view's toolbar — it lists every property you've created, any Camps you belong to, and every active spot, with an archived section tucked below.

The Spots & Properties list — Properties, Camps, and Active Spots each get their own section.
2 Add a spot or a property

Tap the + in the top right. You'll get three choices: Add spot to drop a new pin, New property to create a piece of land to organize spots under, or New camp to start a shared workspace (covered in the Camp section).

The + menu — pick the one that matches what you're setting up.
3 Create a property

Give it a name (your lease name, "Home," a family farm), then set its location on the map. A property's location is pinned once and stays static — every spot inside that property shares this one location for weather and wind, so pin it at the center of the land, not any one stand.

If you've already got spots sitting around ungrouped, you can check them off right here under Add existing spots to fold them into the new property immediately.

Creating a property — name it, pin its location, and optionally sweep in existing spots.
A property's pinned location drives the weather and wind shown for every spot inside it, so accuracy here matters more than it looks like it would.
4 Create a spot

Choose Add spot, name it, and pick a stand type — Box, Ladder, Ground Blind, Climber, Lock-On, Tripod, Food Plot, or Natural. Further down, set the habitat details that actually apply: cover type, water features, terrain, food and attractant sources, and structure or edge features like a funnel or fence line. None of it is required, but the more you fill in, the more Sage has to work with later.

Name and stand type at the top of the form.
Habitat tags and the location picker further down.
5 Set the spot's location

Scroll to the map at the bottom of the New Spot form. Tap Use current to drop the pin where you're standing, pick From saved to reuse a previously saved location, or tap the map itself to open the full-screen location picker and drag the pin to the exact spot.

The full-screen location picker — pan the map until the pin sits exactly where you want it, then Done.
6 Set the spot's optimal wind

Under Optimal Wind (From), tap every compass direction this stand hunts well in. This is separate from live wind data — it's you telling Sage which winds actually work for this stand's setup, so the app can flag when today's forecast wind lines up.

The wind grid on the Edit Spot screen — N is selected here, and Selected/Clear tracks your picks.
You can select more than one direction if a stand hunts well from several winds. Tap Clear to start over.
7 Assign a spot to a property

On the Edit Spot screen, the Property row shows the spot's current assignment — "Not assigned" if it's still ungrouped. Tap Assign to open the property picker: choose an existing property, tap Ungrouped to remove it from all properties, or tap Create new property to build one on the spot without leaving this screen.

The property picker — Ungrouped is always available if a spot doesn't belong under any property.
8 Edit an existing spot

Open any spot's detail page and tap the pencil icon top right. Everything set at creation — name, stand type, optimal wind, property, camera assignment, notes, and every habitat tag — is editable here, and Save writes the changes back immediately.

9 See a spot's full history

Every spot's detail page shows today's score, its best window, and why — a wind cone compass shows the live wind against the stand's optimal directions, and tags like time of day or temperature show which factors are matching in your favor. Below that, log counts for the spot break out into three tabs: Sightings, Logs, and Bucks, each scrollable in place.

Today's score and why, at the top of every spot.
The Logs tab — every hunt logged at this spot.
The Sightings tab — every buck seen here, and when.
The Bucks tab — target bucks associated with this spot.
10 Archive a spot

From the Hunting list, swipe left on a spot and tap Archive Spot. Archiving keeps the spot's history intact but pulls it out of active scoring and off the map — use it for a stand you've pulled or a lease you've dropped, not for one you're just not hunting this week.

Swipe left on any spot in the list to archive it.
Archived spots still show up on the Spots & Properties screen under "Show archived" if you need to bring one back.
